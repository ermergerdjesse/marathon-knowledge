The Why <br />
Rather than creating custom one-off templates for each client or subtle size variants, any incoming client-submitted file can automatically route into one of four standardized sheet layouts based on size.
Before, we had 100s+ of custom cuts at Press Cut; now it's down to 8 - 10 various sizes.

For example, 8.5x8.5" book is cut the same way as an 8x8 book. It is ran through the PUR with the same materials making it more efficient. All of these books have the same exact information on them so it is easier for production to determine the work needed on the one specific book.
Imposition Walkthrough,
Imposition Name: HC_6P_HPMAX_AU_Fabric Cover 85x85
1. Comp Barcode Face A - Datamatrix QR of component Barcode on face of book on side A
2. Job Info Face A - Job information on face of book on side A
   Contains SLA, Order ID, Item ID, Batch, Page Number, and Book Number within Batch
3. Job Info Face B - Job information on face of book on side B
4. Comp Barcode Face B - Datamatrix QR of component Barcode on face of book on side B
5. Item Barcode A - Datamatrix QR of item barcode on side A, specific to Fabric Cover, a cover code attribute is attached with the item barcode, hence Fabric Cover being in name
6. Item Barcode B - Datamatrix QR of item barcode on side B, specific to Fabric Cover, a cover code attribute is attached with the item barcode, hence Fabric Cover being in name
7. Comp Barcode Face A-1 - Datamatrix QR of component barcode on bottom of book on side A
8. Job Info Face A-1 - Job information on bottom of book on side A
   Contains SLA, Order ID, Item ID, Batch, Page Number, and Book Number within Batch
9. Item Barcode A-1 - Datamatrix QR of item barcode on bottom of book side A, specific to Fabric Cover, a cover code attribute is attached with the item barcode, hence Fabric Cover being in name
10. Last Page Mark BR
11. First Page Mark BR
12. Middle Pages Mark BR
13. Infinitrim Barcode
14. Infinitrim Barcode B
15. TLH Press Cut - All press cut marks do not need changed ever. If a bigger or smaller imposition is created, it will follow all of the corners of the artwork which is awesome
16. TLV Press Cut
17. BLH Press Cut
18. BLV Press Cut
19. TRH Press Cut
20. TRV Press Cut
21. BRH Press Cut
22. BRV Press Cut
23. Product Type - HARDCOVER - Red | SOFTCOVER - Blue | TABBED HARDCOVER - Green
24. Final Size - Short Alias at the top right to show the final size of book, sticks out of book to easily sort books post PUR

Before this, the 8.5x8.5 Fabric Cover, 8.5x8.5 Printed Cover, 6x8 Fabric Cover, 6x8 Printed Cover, 8x6 Fabric Cover, 8x6 Printed Cover, 6x6 Printed Cover, 9x9 Printed Cover, etc. all had separate impositions.
Now, there are only 2 different ones which is super fast to change, spending more time checking the changes made, rather than making them so it is more consistent!
