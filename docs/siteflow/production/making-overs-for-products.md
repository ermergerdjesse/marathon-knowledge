# Creating overs on a product in Siteflow, Commercial Work and Formulas
## Why - We create overs for anything with a large quantity. If we ruin one, we do not have to reprint the item and wait for the last trailing item to get to shipping while the other 499 books wait.
### We have to be cautious about this, as printing too many is more of a problem than having less than the order requires
#### Only use these formulas; DO NOT create a new one without consulting Jesse. We have had problems in the past where the formula created doesn't work and it prints 2 of EVERYTHING, which takes months to make up for
---
##### TreeRing (Yearbooks), Partner Plus (Carbooks & Bulk Printing) - Most commonly used formula for overs <br />
IF(AND(itemQuantity>=1,itemQuantity<=19),CEILING(itemQuantity),
IF(AND(itemQuantity>=20,itemQuantity<=49),CEILING(itemQuantity + 2),
IF(AND(itemQuantity>=50,itemQuantity<=74),CEILING(itemQuantity + 4),
IF(AND(itemQuantity>=75,itemQuantity<=99),CEILING(itemQuantity + 6),
IF(AND(itemQuantity>=100,itemQuantity<=499),CEILING(itemQuantity + 10),
IF(AND(itemQuantity>=501,itemQuantity<=10000),CEILING(itemQuantity + 15),
itemQuantity))))))
<br />
Short Description;
<br />
1 - 19 item quantity == No Overs <br />
20 - 49 item quantity == 2 overs <br />
50 - 74 item quantity == 4 overs <br />
75 - 99 item quantity == 6 overs <br />
100 - 499 item quantity == 10 overs <br />
501 - 10000 item quantity == 15 overs <br />
ANYTHING else not above is only the regular order quantity, nothing more or less <br />
This will not print overs for 500 one-off books in one order. This is only for singular item quantities greater than 19.
